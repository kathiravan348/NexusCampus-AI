# NEX-020 — Nexus Assist (M11): Assist panel with sourced mock answers, AI settings, AI-off and consent states

**Status:** done · **Owner:** Claude · **Branch:** task/NEX-020 · **Depends on:** NEX-018
**Requirements:** M11 (L0), A-1–A-8, G-6, D14, D24, ideology §2.3/§2.5

## Goal
Users with AI on open an Assist panel from the shell, ask a question and get a mock answer labelled as AI with its
sources (links to records) and credits used; when AI is off, not entitled, offline, out of credits, or the learner is a
minor without guardian consent, the panel explains why and the workflow still works. Admins manage AI settings.

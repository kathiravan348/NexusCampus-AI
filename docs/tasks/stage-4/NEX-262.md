# NEX-262 — MSW handlers + services: M34, M35 — proctoring, mastery, paths and tutor (read)

## Goal
Demo mode serves the NEX-257 M34 and NEX-221 M35 data through MSW read endpoints with tenant isolation, deny-by-default
permissions and row filtering, and `@nexus/services` has typed hooks for them.

## Build
1. `db.ts`: collections for `PROCTORING_SETTINGS`, `PROCTORING_SESSIONS`, `RECORDING_SEGMENTS`, `PROCTORING_FLAGS`,
   `MASTERY_RECORDS`, `LEARNING_PATHS`, `TUTOR_SETTINGS`, `TUTOR_SESSIONS`, `TUTOR_MESSAGES` (pattern of NEX-223).
2. Handlers: GET only, `get()` from `route.ts`, `list()` pages, `ofTenant` always, `entitled()` per module (M34 only
   for tenants entitled to it — Northbridge, Apex), 404 for another tenant's id; files ≤ 300 lines; no personal data
   in errors.
   M34: `/online-tests/:id/proctoring` (settings) and `/proctoring-sessions?testId=` with `m34.proctoring.manage`;
   `/proctoring-sessions/:id/flags` and `/proctoring-sessions/:id/recordings` (metadata only) with
   `m34.flag.review` or `m34.proctoring.manage`. Learners and guardians: no M34 grant → 403 (their own notice and
   session come with the M34 screen task).
   M35: `/mastery?learnerId=&courseSpaceId=` and `/learning-paths?learnerId=` with `m35.mastery.read`, limited to
   learners of course spaces the viewer is staff of (admin and owner: all of the tenant); `/course-spaces/:id/tutor`
   (settings) with `m35.tutor.manage`; `/tutor-sessions?courseSpaceId=` and `/tutor-sessions/:id/messages` with
   `m35.transcript.read` (staff of the space; admin all). Learners and guardians have no M35 grant yet → 403.
3. Services: one `useX()` hook per endpoint (pattern of `modules/m33.ts`, keys in `keys.ts`), exported from `index.ts`.

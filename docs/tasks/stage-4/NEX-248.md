# NEX-248 — Contracts (M46): engagement snapshots, risk scores with factors, interventions

**Status:** ready-for-review · **Owner:** Claude · **Branch:** task/NEX-248 · **Depends on:** NEX-213, NEX-214, NEX-247

## Goal
`@nexus/contracts` defines learning analytics and early warning: engagement per learner, an AI risk score that always
shows its main factors and waits for a person's review, and the interventions a person plans — never an automatic action.

## Build
1. `EngagementSnapshotSchema` `{ tenantId, learnerId, courseSpaceId (null = all courses), periodStart, periodEnd
   (IsoDate, end ≥ start), logins, activeMinutes, itemsCompleted, submissionsOnTime, submissionsLate,
   submissionsMissing (ints ≥ 0), attendanceBps (0–10000, nullable; from M17), averageScoreBps (0–10000, nullable),
   computedAt }`.
2. `RiskFactorCodeSchema` `attendance_drop | missing_work | late_work | low_scores | score_decline | low_engagement`;
   `RiskFactorSchema` `{ code, weightBps (1–10000), detail (1–300; plain words, no health or safeguarding data),
   sourceModule (ModuleId, one of M17 M20 M30 M32 M33 M35) }`.
3. `RiskLevelSchema` `low | medium | high`; `RiskReviewStatusSchema` `open | confirmed | dismissed`; `RiskScoreSchema`
   `{ id, tenantId, learnerId, aiGenerated: literal true, tier (CapabilityTier), modelVersion (1–64), scoreBps
   (0–10000), level, factors (1–8, unique codes, Σ weightBps ≤ 10000, sorted by weight high → low), computedAt,
   status, reviewedByStaffProfileId + reviewedAt (set iff not open, reviewedAt ≥ computedAt), reviewNote (≤ 1000,
   nullable; required when dismissed) }`.
4. `InterventionKindSchema` `meeting | tutoring | family_contact | learning_plan | counselling_referral | other`;
   `InterventionStatusSchema` `planned | in_progress | completed | cancelled`; `InterventionSchema` `{ id, tenantId,
   learnerId, riskScoreId (nullable — staff may act without a score), kind, title (1–200), plan (≤ 2000; personal
   data), ownerStaffProfileId, createdByStaffProfileId, status, startOn, reviewOn (nullable, ≥ startOn), outcome
   (≤ 2000, nullable; required when completed; personal data), createdAt, updatedAt }`.
5. Doc comment (A-3, A-4, V-7): risk scores are read only with an M46 staff permission and never shown to learners or
   families; no score starts an intervention, message or record change by itself; factors never use health (M61) or
   safeguarding (M62) data; a confirmed score needs a person; the server keeps the model inputs for audit.
6. `updatedAt >= createdAt`. Strict objects, `XSchema` + `type X`, named exports, ≤ 300 lines, no workspace imports.

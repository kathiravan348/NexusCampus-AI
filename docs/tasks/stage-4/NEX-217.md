## Build
1. `IsbnSchema`: ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 digits only (hyphens removed by the client) with a valid check digit.
2. `CatalogueKindSchema` `book | ebook | journal | audio | video | digital_resource`; `CatalogueRecordSchema` `{ id,
   tenantId, institutionId, kind, title (1–500), authors (≤ 20 of 1–200), isbn (nullable), publisher (≤ 200, nullable),
   year (1000–2100, nullable), language (Locale), subjects (≤ 20 of 1–100), classification (≤ 50, nullable), summary
   (≤ 4000), digitalAccess (`{ provider (settings key), externalId (1–200) }`, set iff ebook | digital_resource), source
   (manual | isbn_lookup | ai_photo), confirmedByStaffProfileId (set iff source ≠ manual), createdAt, updatedAt }`.
3. `LibraryCopySchema` `{ id, tenantId, recordId, barcode (`^[A-Z0-9-]{4,32}$`), campusId (nullable), shelf (≤ 50,
   nullable), status (available | on_loan | on_hold | in_repair | lost | withdrawn), acquiredOn (nullable), price
   (MinorUnits ≥ 0) + currency (both or neither) }`.
4. `LoanSchema` `{ id, tenantId, copyId, borrowerProfileId, borrowerKind (learner | staff), issuedAt, dueOn, renewals
   (0–10), status (active | overdue | returned | lost), returnedAt (set iff returned, ≥ issuedAt),
   issuedByStaffProfileId, clientId (UUID; offline L2), deviceId (nullable) }`; `dueOn` not before the issue date.
5. `HoldSchema` `{ id, tenantId, recordId, borrowerProfileId, placedAt, position (≥ 1), status (waiting | ready |
   fulfilled | cancelled | expired), readyUntil (set iff ready, > placedAt) }`.
6. `LibraryFineSchema` `{ id, tenantId, loanId, borrowerProfileId, reason (overdue | lost | damage), amount (MinorUnits
   > 0), currency, status (open | invoiced | paid | waived), feeInvoiceId (set iff invoiced | paid; M18),
   waivedByStaffProfileId + waiveReason (1–300) (set iff waived) }`.
7. `ReadingRecommendationSchema` `{ tenantId, borrowerProfileId, recordId, reason (1–300, A-5), aiGenerated: literal
   true, tier, createdAt }`.
8. Doc comment (G-6, V-7, A-3): reading history is private — only the borrower and library staff see past loans;
   guardians of a minor see current loans and fines only; returned loans are anonymised after the tenant's retention
   period (fines kept); recommendations need the tenant AI switch and a guardian's `ai_features` consent for minors and
   use only the borrower's own history; region packs may forbid fines for minors. `updatedAt >= createdAt`; strict
   objects, named exports, ≤ 300 lines.

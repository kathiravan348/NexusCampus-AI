   framework (`institutionId` null) with parent and child outcomes, syllabus units for 2 subjects, lesson plans in every
   status (one `ai_draft`), coverage for 2 offerings. Northbridge: course-outcome and program-outcome frameworks with
   ≥ 4 CO → PO mappings. Apex: exam-syllabus standards. Harmony: a skills framework with graded levels.

   (every kind at least once except `assignment`, `test`, `live_session` — see step 6; `content` items point at
   `CONTENT_ITEMS`; links https), `COURSE_PROGRESS` (learners of the space's class groups), `DISCUSSION_THREADS`, `DISCUSSION_POSTS` (one reply, one hidden by a teacher).
4. `content.ts`: `CONTENT_ASSETS`, `CONTENT_ITEMS` (a lesson with every block type except `question`, a captioned
   video, an audio with a transcript, a SCORM 2004 and a cmi5 package; published items have a clean accessibility check; one draft lesson has
   a `missing_alt_text` error to show the publish block), `CONTENT_ATTEMPTS` (each status).
5. Staff are the tenant's `rp-…-staff` role profiles; learners come from `LEARNERS`; dates inside each tenant's calendar
   year; `CREATED_AT` / `UPDATED_AT` from `tenants.ts`; fictional, non-identifying text; files ≤ 300 lines.
6. Deferred (no M32/M33/M36 records here): `assignment` / `test` items and the `question` block come in NEX-221,

## Goal
`@nexus/contracts` defines special education and inclusion records — IEP / ILP plans with the family's participation,
measurable goals that a person approves (AI drafts only suggest), accommodations that tests and exams honour, support
sessions and goal progress — as restricted personal data.

## Build
1. `SupportPlanKindSchema` `iep | ilp | accommodation_plan | other`; `SupportPlanSchema` `{ id, tenantId, learnerId,
   kind, status (draft | active | under_review | closed), startOn, reviewOn (≥ startOn), endOn (nullable, ≥ startOn;
   set iff closed), coordinatorStaffProfileId, teamStaffProfileIds (unique, 1–20, includes the coordinator), guardian
   (invited | agreed | declined | not_applicable), guardianRespondedOn (set iff agreed | declined), strengths (≤ 4000),
   needsSummary (≤ 4000; restricted — never sent to AI, exports for others or logs), createdAt, updatedAt }`.
2. `SupportGoalAreaSchema` `communication | literacy | numeracy | social_emotional | motor | independence | other`;
   `SupportGoalSchema` `{ id, tenantId, planId, area, statement (1–1000), measure (1–500), targetDate, outcomeIds
   (unique, ≤ 10), source (manual | ai_draft), status (draft | active | achieved | discontinued),
   approvedByStaffProfileId + approvedAt (set iff not draft) }` — an `ai_draft` goal is active only after a person
   approves it (A-4).
3. `AccommodationCategorySchema` `extra_time | separate_room | reader | scribe | assistive_technology | rest_breaks |
   modified_materials | seating | other`; `AccommodationSchema` `{ id, tenantId, planId, category, details (≤ 500),
   appliesTo (classroom | tests | exams | all), extraTimePercent (1–100, set iff extra_time), active, startOn, endOn
   (nullable, ≥ startOn) }`.
4. `SupportSessionSchema` `{ id, tenantId, planId, goalId (nullable), date, minutes (1–600), deliveredByStaffProfileId,
   note (≤ 2000, nullable) }`; `GoalProgressSchema` `{ id, tenantId, goalId, recordedOn, rating (not_started |
   emerging | progressing | achieved), note (≤ 2000, nullable), recordedByStaffProfileId }`.
5. Doc comment (G-6, A-3, V-7): every M38 record is restricted — read only by the plan team, staff holding the M38
   permission and the learner's guardians (and the learner where the institution allows); `needsSummary` is health-like
   data and never reaches the AI gateway; AI goal drafts are off unless the institution switches them on and use only
   the goal area, outcomes and progress ratings; M21 / M33 read active accommodations to adjust time and rooms.
6. `updatedAt >= createdAt`. Strict objects, `XSchema` + `type X`, named exports, ≤ 300 lines, no workspace imports.

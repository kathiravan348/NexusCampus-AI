## Build

2. `early-years.ts` (Little Steps only): `DIARY_ENTRIES` (every kind, captured by staff), `OBSERVATIONS` (one of 2
   children), `MILESTONE_PROGRESS` (each status, milestones = NEX-220 Little Steps outcomes), `PICKUP_CHECKS` (each
   collector kind and verification; one refused, collector not on `AUTHORISED_PICKUPS`, restricted reason),
   `RATIO_SNAPSHOTS` (one below the required ratio), `DAY_SUMMARIES` (one AI draft not yet sent, one sent by a person —
   only for children whose guardian granted `ai_features` in `CONSENTS`). No medication or health text anywhere.
3. `inclusion.ts` (Riverside and Northbridge): `SUPPORT_PLANS` (each kind and status; one awaiting guardian agreement),
   `SUPPORT_GOALS` (each status; one AI draft with its original wording and the person's decision — A-4),
   `ACCOMMODATIONS` (one extra-time for M21 / M33), `SUPPORT_SESSIONS`, `GOAL_PROGRESS` (each rating). `needsSummary`
   is short, generic and non-diagnostic (e.g. "Benefits from reading support") — never a condition name.
4. `portfolios.ts` (Little Steps and Riverside): `LEARNER_MEDIA_CONSENTS` (each status; match `CONSENTS` purpose
   `media_sharing` where one exists), `MEDIA_ITEMS` (each visibility; `consentState` computed by the header rule — one
   `cleared`, one `blocked` because a tagged learner opted out, one `pending`; one AI caption labelled as AI, naming
   nobody), `PORTFOLIOS`, `PORTFOLIO_ENTRIES` (every kind; one reflection), `PORTFOLIO_EXPORTS` (each status, no URL).
5. Staff are the tenant's `rp-…-staff` role profiles; learners from `LEARNERS`, guardians from `GUARDIAN_RELATIONSHIPS`;
   dates inside each tenant's calendar year; `CREATED_AT` / `UPDATED_AT` from `tenants.ts`; fictional, non-identifying
   text; files ≤ 300 lines.
6. D87 rule 5: these sets are imported by path, not added to `fixtures/index.ts` (NEX-221 holds that file; NEX-224 adds
   the exports for MSW). Not in `demoFixtures()`, no `DEMO_FIXTURES_VERSION` bump, nothing in `backend/`.

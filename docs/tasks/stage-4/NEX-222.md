- The listed files name the computed `SyllabusCoverage` response but do not define its schema or fields. May Codex read the M29–M31 contract definitions, or can the task list the required coverage response shape and calculation rules?
  - answered by the planner: Yes — read `frontend/packages/contracts/src/{curriculum,courses,content}.ts` (read-only)
    for every M29–M31 shape. `SyllabusCoverageSchema` is in `curriculum.ts`. `/syllabus-coverage?subjectOfferingId=`
    returns `{ coverage: SyllabusCoverage, entries: CoverageEntry[] }` (parse it in the service with a local
    `z.strictObject` of those two schemas; no contract change). `topicsTotal` = topics of the tenant's `SYLLABUS_UNITS`
    with the offering's `subjectId` and its class group's `yearLevelId`; `topicsCovered` = that offering's coverage
    entries with status `covered` whose topic is in that set; `percentBps` by the schema's rounding rule (0 when no
    topics); `entries` = all coverage entries of the offering. Unknown or other-tenant offering → 404.

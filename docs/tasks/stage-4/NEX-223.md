## Build
1. `stage4-access.ts`: add `m32.assignment.read` to the `learner` system role only (planner decision: learners read
   and later submit their own assignments; every row is still filtered to their own). No other grant changes.
2. `db.ts`: new collections for every NEX-221 M32 / M33 set (pattern of the NEX-220 collections).
3. Handlers: GET only, `get()` from `route.ts`, `list()` pages, `ofTenant` always, `entitled()` per module, 404 for
   another tenant's id; files ≤ 300 lines (split into `m33Store.ts` if needed); no personal data in errors.
   M32 (`m32.assignment.read`): `/assignments?courseSpaceId=`, `/assignments/:id`, `/assignments/:id/submissions`,
   `/submissions/:id/feedback`, `/submissions/:id/peer-reviews`, `/submissions/:id/originality`,
   `/submissions/:id/ai-suggestions`. Without `m32.assignment.manage`: published / closed assignments of course spaces
   the viewer is staff or a learner of (NEX-222 rule); a learner sees only submissions where they are `learnerId` or in
   `groupLearnerIds`, feedback only with `releasedAt` set; staff of the space see all. An anonymous peer review read by
   the author or a group member has `reviewerLearnerId: null`. AI suggestions and originality only with
   `m32.submission.mark`.
   M33 (`m33.test.read`): `/question-banks`, `/question-items?bankId=`, `/online-tests?courseSpaceId=`,
   `/online-tests/:id`, `/online-tests/:id/attempts`, `/attempts/:id/responses`, `/online-tests/:id/item-statistics`.
   Banks and items only with `m33.bank.manage` (learners never get `QuestionItem`). Tests: without `m33.test.manage`
   only non-draft tests of the viewer's spaces. Attempts and responses: learner own rows only; staff of the space all.
   Item statistics only with `m33.test.manage`. A learner's responses hide scores until the test's `showResults`
   allows (`immediately`; `after_close` once closed; `manual` never in this task).
4. Services: one `useX()` hook per endpoint (pattern of `modules/m30.ts`, keys in `keys.ts`), exported from `index.ts`.

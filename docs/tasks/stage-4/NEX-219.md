# NEX-219 — Contracts (M42): credit requirements, elective registration, degree audit, thesis supervision, AI path advice

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** — · **Branch:** task/NEX-219 · **Depends on:** NEX-146
**Requirements:** M42, G-3, O (L1), A-4, A-5, D87

## Goal
`@nexus/contracts` defines higher-ed academics across credit systems (CBCS, ECTS, semester hours): programme credit
requirements, elective registration windows and choices decided by staff, a degree audit whose verdict follows from its
numbers, thesis supervision and AI degree-path advice an advisor reviews.

## Build
1. Credits are numbers 0–300 in 0.5 steps. `CreditSystemSchema` `cbcs | ects | semester_hours | custom`;
   `ProgramRequirementSchema` `{ id, tenantId, programId, name, category (core | elective | open_elective |
   ability_enhancement | skill | project | other), creditSystem, minCredits, maxCredits (nullable, ≥ min), subjectIds
   (unique, ≤ 200), effectiveFromYearId }`.
2. `RegistrationWindowSchema` `{ id, tenantId, programId, yearLevelId (nullable), academicYearId, periodId (nullable),
   opensAt, closesAt (> opensAt), minCredits, maxCredits (≥ min), status (draft | open | closed) }`;
   `ElectiveRegistrationSchema` `{ id, tenantId, windowId, learnerId, choices (1–20 of `{ subjectOfferingId, priority
   (1–20) }`, unique offerings and priorities), status (submitted | confirmed | waitlisted | rejected | withdrawn),
   decidedByStaffProfileId + decidedAt (set iff confirmed | rejected), clientId (UUID), submittedAt }`.
3. `RequirementProgressSchema` `{ requirementId, creditsEarned, creditsRequired, satisfied (= earned ≥ required) }`;
   `DegreeAuditSchema` `{ id, tenantId, learnerId, programId, computedAt, creditsEarned, creditsRequired, gpa (nullable,
   0…gpaScale), gpaScale (4 | 5 | 10), requirements (1–50, unique ids), eligibleToGraduate (= every requirement
   satisfied and earned ≥ required) }` — a person still confirms graduation (M22 / M86).
4. `ThesisStageSchema` `proposal | in_progress | submitted | under_examination | revisions | accepted | withdrawn`;
   `ThesisSchema` `{ id, tenantId, learnerId, title (1–300), supervisorStaffProfileIds (unique, 1–3), stage, milestones
   (≤ 20 of `{ name (1–200), dueOn, completedOn (nullable) }`), externalExaminerName (≤ 200, nullable; personal data),
   originalitySignalId (nullable; M32), createdAt, updatedAt }`.
5. `PathAdviceSchema` `{ id, tenantId, learnerId, aiGenerated: literal true, tier, suggestions (1–10 of `{ subjectId,
   reason (1–300) }`), warnings (≤ 5 of 1–300), status (draft | reviewed), reviewedByStaffProfileId + reviewedAt (set iff
   reviewed), createdAt }` — advice never registers a learner (A-4).
6. `updatedAt >= createdAt`. Strict objects, `XSchema` + `type X`, named exports, ≤ 300 lines, no workspace imports.

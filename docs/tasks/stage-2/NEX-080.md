# NEX-080 — P09 Support desk & SLAs

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** — · **Branch:** task/NEX-080 · **Depends on:** NEX-079
**Requirements:** P09, V-3 (access only via institution approval), V-6 (diagnostic bundles), A-1

## Goal
Staff with `ctl.ticket.read` open **Support**: tickets with priority, status, SLA due time (breached ones stand
out) and assignee, and the SLA targets per support tier. A ticket page shows the thread (customer replies, staff
replies, internal notes, AI-drafted labels) and any linked access request. Staff with `ctl.ticket.reply` answer or
add an internal note; answering assigns an unassigned ticket to them.

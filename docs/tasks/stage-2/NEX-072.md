## Goal
Staff with `ctl.account.read` open **Accounts**: a searchable, filterable list of customers and an account page with
contacts, opportunities, contracts and notes; staff with `ctl.account.manage` add notes. Partner users see only the
accounts their partner manages (server-side, NEX-070).

1. List: name, type (`m01.type.*`), model, stage badge, learners, health (≥ 70 healthy, 50–69 watch, < 50 at risk),
   renewal date; search by name, filters stage + model; row opens the account.
2. Account page: summary (country, region `m01.region.*`, owner, partner, tenants, since) + Tabs. Money and dates via
   `@nexus/i18n` formatters in the staff member's language.

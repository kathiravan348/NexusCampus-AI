# NEX-096 — Control backend skeleton: own app, own database, health, staff `me`

**Status:** in-progress · **Owner:** Claude · **Branch:** task/NEX-096 · **Depends on:** NEX-095
**Requirements:** ARCHITECTURE §9 (control backend + control DB, metadata only), P13, V-7, D50, D55

## Goal
Nexus Control has its own backend app (`nexus.control.main:create_app`, port 8100) with its **own database**
(`nexus_control`, own app role `control_app`, own migrations) that never touches the product database. It answers
`GET /control/api/v1/health`, `/ready` and `/me` (the `StaffSession` contract). Until staff SSO (next task) a dev-only
header `X-Nexus-Staff` picks the staff member, and only when `environment` is `dev` or `test`; in `prod` `/me` is 401.
Staff and role permissions come from the same fixtures as the mocks (exported JSON, parity-tested).

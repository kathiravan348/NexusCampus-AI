## Goal
Staff with `ctl.release.read` open **Releases**: every release with channel, status, signature, offline bundle and
minimum upgrade version; choosing one shows its migration notes and rollout rings (0 our own cell → 3 everyone).
Staff with `ctl.release.manage` sign and publish a draft (AI-drafted notes need a person's confirmation) and start
the next pending ring (deployments in it move to that version).

**Status:** done · **Owner:** Claude · **Branch:** task/NEX-081 · **Depends on:** NEX-080
**Requirements:** P10, V-1, V-3, V-5, D22, M83

## Goal
Staff with `ctl.broker.read` open **Access requests**: our requests to institutions (status decided by the
institution), time-boxed recorded sessions, and break-glass cases (two approvers, institution notified, report owed or
delivered). Staff with `ctl.broker.request` ask an institution for access: tenant, modules (never M83), read or edit,
1–72 hours, reason, optional ticket. In the demo the request lands in the institution's own **Data vault & access**
(M83) list, and its decision there shows up here.

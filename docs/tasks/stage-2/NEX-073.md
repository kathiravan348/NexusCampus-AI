# NEX-073 — P02 Plans, pricing & quotes

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** — · **Branch:** task/NEX-073 · **Depends on:** NEX-072
**Requirements:** P02, A-1 (AI labelled, human applies), D14, D51, D52, D53

## Goal
Staff with `ctl.quote.read` open **Pricing & quotes**: the catalogue (SaaS plans, add-ons, AI credit packs, licence
editions, support) with prices per country, and quotes with totals. Staff with `ctl.quote.manage` send a draft quote;
an **AI-drafted** quote can only be sent after a person ticks that they checked it (human in the loop).

# NEX-074 — P03 Subscription billing

**Status:** planned · **Owner:** — · **Branch:** task/NEX-074 · **Depends on:** NEX-073
**Requirements:** P03, D23 (billing never deletes data), D51, D53

## Goal
Staff with `ctl.billing.read` open **Billing**: subscriptions, invoices (GST / VAT / none), payments (gateway
reference only, never card or bank numbers) and dunning. Staff with `ctl.billing.manage` record a bank transfer that
pays an open or overdue invoice.

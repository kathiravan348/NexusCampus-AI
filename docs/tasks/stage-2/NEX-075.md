**Status:** done · **Owner:** Claude · **Branch:** task/NEX-075 · **Depends on:** NEX-074

## Goal
Staff with `ctl.provisioning.read` open **Provisioning**: new-tenant jobs with their steps (tenant, keys, profile,
seed, owner invite) and offboarding jobs ending in a signed key-destruction certificate. Staff with
`ctl.provisioning.manage` start a trial tenant for an account and move a job through its steps (demo). The step list
is the first shared Control component (`JobSteps`, also used by P18).
